Select Sideline is a curated youth football playbook for select-age teams in 3rd through 8th grade. The job is to make the next play easy to teach, keep a roster next to play calling, and work offline on a phone or tablet. It is free.

This is not a blank designer. You do not start from an empty field and invent a system from scratch. The plays are already chosen for youth select football, so a coach can teach them and call them on Friday night.

This is also not a film platform. We are not trying to replace Hudl or a video room. Select Sideline is for the sideline: who is on, what you are calling, and a sheet you can print.

If you coach select or premier youth, open the live product at [https://selectsideline.com](https://selectsideline.com).
